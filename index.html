Project summary

Vektor is a cart-based differential-drive platform for autonomous navigation indoors. Computing runs on Ubuntu 22.04 (Jetson). Software uses ROS 2 Humble, RTAB-Map SLAM fed by RGB-D and laser-style scans, Nav2 for planning and recovery, DepthAI Luxonis cameras, and fused odometry plus safety monitoring (ultrasonics, cliffs).
